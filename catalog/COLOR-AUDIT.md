Catatan audit:

- Segi4 Motif Tryspan: All active July and older series color posters reviewed. SOLD folders excluded. Primrose and Cielo use descriptive visible-color names because the sources do not supply shade names. Zaina/Tania source alias retained. Misfiled Makkah Pink identified by its printed title.
- Zoya Instan: 13 named fabric colors. Repeated model/promotional images are not additional colors. Black filename and full chart identify black; single-photo label says smoke in error.
- Paris Japan: Names transcribed from the images, including Sheer Blue, Fresh Milo and Almond Beige. Charcoal is chart-only; Granola is individual-photo-only.
- Hawa Instan: Paris Jadul Mustard is misfiled in the Hawa folder and excluded. Dark Beige occurs on the labelled color chart.
- Mukena Armani Velvet: Eight active Velvet volumes reviewed, Vol. 1 SOLD excluded. Solid Pink Vol. 2 has two different skirt colors. Source aliases retained for Vol. 3 Denim/Storm and Vol. 5 Sea/Blue, Pale/Pink. Different volumes remain distinct choices.
- Lea Oval Khiban: All 26 root photos reviewed. White and pink confirmed. Short Zea photos are misfiled and excluded.
- Zea Sport Instan: All 22 root photos reviewed. Pink and taupe confirmed. White photos depict Lea and are excluded.
- Ciput / Inner Tali: No matching, verifiable color photography found in the supplied source tree; keep explicit photo/color inquiry.
- Armani Lasercut: No matching, verifiable color photography found in the supplied source tree; keep explicit photo/color inquiry.
- Armani Panel Printing: No matching, verifiable color photography found in the supplied source tree; keep explicit photo/color inquiry.

- Mukena Armani Ori Raya: Active older Armani Ori collections are distinct from the Velvet catalog. No retail price is inferred from Lasercut or Panel Printing. Vol. 2 source folder explicitly says only child sizes remain; adult versions and SOLD OUT volumes excluded.
